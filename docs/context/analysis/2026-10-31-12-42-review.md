# Rules hardening — weakness analysis

**Scope:** D4 (decision pre-mortem), the exit clause, D5 (proposed: mechanical or visible), and the closing protocol introduced in this session. Weaknesses are classified by **current implementation** and **future resolvability**, replacing severity ordering (which does not terminate).

## 1. Executive summary

Thirteen weaknesses emerged across two sessions. The classification is **bimodal**: either "not done + mechanically solvable today" (0+M) or "partial + judgment-bound or structurally unresolvable" (2+J/X). No item is "partial + mechanically solvable today". The mechanical backlog is short (one-line fixes); the rest is the correct boundary of a self-audit, not a failure of D4.

**Structural finding:** the exit clause of D4 is itself a self-controlled condition — the leak D4's second question is designed to catch. Same pattern as H3 (deferred red item), P1 (`Rules-Audit: skipped`), L-G (`DPs applied:` in prose). Fix: replace judgment with objective classification, and make every deferral visible via the `Deferred:` trailer.

## 3. Actions and retrospective classification

### 3a. Protocol weaknesses (A–M)

These are defects of the protocol, not findings: they are fixed or declared, not classified as pure/gap.

- **A, B**: D4 amended (self-application; "operational" defined).
- **C, D, F**: declared as residual risks in `AGENT-POLICY.md` §9.
- **E**: superseded. The "aging" mechanism is not introduced (D3): a finding tracked with an executable first action is a gap; tracking happens in `SESSION-STATE.md` and is read at every startup (E1).
- **H, L**: mechanical fixes.
- **K**: covered by the `Rules-Audit:` trailer format.
- **M**: declared here. No new trailer: too rare for a mechanism (D3).
- **G**: phase 2 (PR review).
- **I, J**: accepted.

D4's blind spot is inherent to any self-audit. The fix is not a better D4 but: **D5** (visible artifact) + **objective exit classification** + **maintainer post-push review**, mediated by the **Filter agent** (D4: judgment-bound gaps are presented once, in pause, then tracked if undecided).
